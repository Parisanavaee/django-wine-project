## Replacing with your own photography

Drop your own JPG / WebP / AVIF imagery at the same paths and the layout
will absorb it. Recommended dimensions:

- Hero bottle (vertical): 1600 × 2000 px (4:5), AVIF / WebP, ≤ 250 kB
- Wine cards: 1200 × 1500 px (4:5), AVIF / WebP, ≤ 200 kB
- Long table & pairing (wide): 1600 × 900 px (16:9), ≤ 250 kB
- Experience tiles: 1400 × 1050 px (4:3), ≤ 250 kB
- Estate hero / aerial: 1600 × 1067 px (3:2), ≤ 280 kB
- Portraits: 800 × 1000 px (4:5), ≤ 120 kB
- Journal cards: 1200 × 900 px (4:3), ≤ 180 kB
