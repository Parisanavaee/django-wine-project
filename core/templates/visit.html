Before you come · short FAQ

The questions we are asked the most.

If your question is not here, write to Camila — the email is on the contact page.

Q · 01

Is there parking at the estate?

Yes — eight spaces along the eastern fence. Most guests drive themselves. If you prefer not to drive after a tasting, we can arrange a car back to Mendoza for €60.

Q · 02

Can we buy wine at the estate?

Yes. The full current release is for sale at the tasting room. Library wines are by appointment — write ahead and we will set aside what you would like to see.

Q · 03

Is the estate dog-friendly?

Outside, yes — Hilda and Mario will be delighted. Inside the cellar and tasting room we ask that pets stay on the terrace.

Q · 04

Can we stay overnight?

We do not have rooms on the estate, but we are happy to suggest two small hotels nearby that we love — write to us with the dates and we will send the names.

Q · 05

Are children welcome?

At the Sunday long table and on the harvest day, yes. Cellar dinners are adults only.

Q · 06

Can we shoot photography on the estate?

For editorial and personal use, yes — just write ahead. For commercial use, write to press@vermora.example.
